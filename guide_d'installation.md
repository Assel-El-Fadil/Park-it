# Installation Guide

This guide walks you through installing the app on your Android or iOS device.

---

## Android

### Step 1 — Download the APK

Download the `.apk` file sent to you onto your Android phone.

> **Tip:** If you're downloading from a link, tap it directly in your browser. The file will appear in your **Downloads** folder.

---

### Step 2 — Allow installation from unknown sources

Because this app is not installed from the Play Store, you need to allow your phone to install it manually. The steps vary slightly by Android version:

**Android 8 and above:**

1. Open your phone's **Settings**
2. Go to **Apps** → **Special app access** → **Install unknown apps**
3. Find your browser or file manager (whichever you used to download the file)
4. Toggle **Allow from this source** to ON

**Older Android versions:**

1. Open **Settings**
2. Go to **Security**
3. Enable **Unknown sources**

---

### Step 3 — Install the APK

1. Open your **Downloads** folder (or use a file manager app)
2. Tap the `.apk` file
3. Tap **Install** when prompted
4. Wait for the installation to complete
5. Tap **Open** to launch the app

---

### Troubleshooting (Android)

| Problem                     | Solution                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Install blocked" warning   | Follow Step 2 again and make sure the correct app is allowed |
| App not found after install | Check your app drawer or search for the app name             |
| "Parsing error"             | The APK file may be corrupted — re-download and try again    |

## iOS

### Before you begin

iOS does not allow direct APK installs like Android. Instead, you will install the app using a tool called **AltStore**, which lets you install apps directly from your computer.

You will need:

- A **Mac or Windows PC**
- A **USB cable** to connect your iPhone
- Your **Apple ID** (a free account is fine)
- The `.ipa` file sent to you

---

### Step 1 — Install AltStore on your computer

1. Go to [https://altstore.io](https://altstore.io) on your computer
2. Download **AltServer** for Mac or Windows
3. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions

**On Mac:** Move AltServer to your Applications folder and open it. It will appear in your menu bar.

**On Windows:** AltServer runs in your system tray (bottom-right of your taskbar). You may also need to install **iTunes** and **iCloud** from Apple's website if you don't have them already.

---

### Step 2 — Install AltStore on your iPhone

1. Connect your iPhone to your computer with a USB cable
2. Trust the computer on your iPhone if prompted
3. **On Mac:** Click the AltServer icon in the menu bar → **Install AltStore** → select your iPhone
4. **On Windows:** Click the AltServer icon in the system tray → **Install AltStore** → select your iPhone
5. Enter your **Apple ID** and password when asked

> **Note:** Your Apple ID credentials are only used to sign the app locally on your device. They are not sent anywhere else.

6. Wait for AltStore to appear on your iPhone's home screen

---

### Step 3 — Trust the app on your iPhone

1. On your iPhone, go to **Settings** → **General** → **VPN & Device Management**
2. Tap your Apple ID email address under **Developer App**
3. Tap **Trust** and confirm

---

### Step 4 — Install the app

1. Copy the `.ipa` file to your iPhone (via AirDrop, email, or Files app)
2. Open **AltStore** on your iPhone
3. Tap the **+** button in the top-left corner
4. Select the `.ipa` file
5. Enter your Apple ID and password if prompted
6. Wait for the installation to complete — the app will appear on your home screen

---

### Keeping the app active (iOS only)

iOS apps installed via AltStore expire after **7 days** unless refreshed. To prevent the app from stopping:

1. Connect your iPhone to the same Wi-Fi as your computer (or use USB)
2. Make sure AltServer is running on your computer
3. Open **AltStore** on your iPhone → tap **My Apps**
4. Tap **Refresh All**

You can also enable **Background Refresh** in AltStore settings so this happens automatically.

---

### Troubleshooting (iOS)

| Problem                              | Solution                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AltStore not appearing after install | Restart your iPhone and check the home screen                                 |
| "Untrusted Developer" error          | Follow Step 3 to trust the app in Settings                                    |
| App expires and won't open           | Refresh it in AltStore (see above)                                            |
| AltServer can't find my iPhone       | Make sure iTunes/iCloud is installed and try a different USB cable            |
| Install fails with an error code     | Make sure you are using the correct Apple ID and that your iPhone is unlocked |
